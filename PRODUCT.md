## Platform

web

## Users
Physiotherapists, physiotherapy students and electrotherapy practitioners in Portugal (pt-PT). Most arrive from Instagram/Facebook Reels on a phone, mid-scroll, after seeing an ad that showed an electrode map in detail. Their job: know exactly where to place electrodes and which parameters to use for a given condition, with a source behind it.

## Product Purpose
Sell "Atlas de Posicionamento de Elétrodos · Edição Inverno" (ElectroMapas), a 397-page PDF, for a one-off 9,97 € via Hotmart. Success = InitiateCheckout -> Purchase on the Hotmart link, with UTM/SCK/SRC attribution intact.

## Positioning
Every one of the 70 electrode maps was checked against its original study (PMID), and the atlas shows its own verification log map by map, including what was not confirmed. A competitor could copy the drawings, not the audit trail.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- PDF, 397 pages, A4 landscape; best on tablet/computer, readable on phone with zoom.
- 70 maps; each = map page (CH1/CH2 by colour, placement, study parameters) + ficha prática (step by step, what the patient feels, when to stop, common errors, warning signs, session log).
- Winter chapter (cold and pain, heat/cryotherapy, dry skin and electrodes, falls); 46 clinical guides by condition (NICE/Cochrane); currents and parameters (TENS, NMES, IFC, Russian, Aussie, FES) with quick cards; anatomy and motor points (58 pages); safety, consent and 11 scale record sheets; index by condition; full bibliography; verification log.
- Delivery by email via Hotmart. Payment MB WAY, Multibanco, card. 15-day Hotmart guarantee.
- Educational material with references; does not replace individual assessment.
- Post-purchase upsell (Pacote PRO) exists and is never mentioned on the sales page.
